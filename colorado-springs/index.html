Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Colorado Springs

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep shrubs and grass trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and have a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still inexpensive.

How old is your system? Its age says a lot about what comes next. With regular service, most air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is past that point and is also showing rising bills, uneven temperatures, or frequent repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and less stressful than an emergency replacement when the first heat wave hits.

If comfort problems do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A weak capacitor is a quick fix.
